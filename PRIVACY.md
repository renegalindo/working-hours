# Privacy policy

*Working Hours, Chrome extension. Last updated 19 October 2026.*

Working Hours collects no personal data. Nothing you do with it is sent to the developer or anyone else.

## What it reads

The extension runs only on `calendar.google.com`. There it reads the layout of Calendar's time grid: which date each day column shows, how tall the column is, and the page's background colour, so it can draw shading behind the right hours in a matching tint. It does not read your events, their titles or details, your contacts, or anything else on the page. All of this happens inside your browser.

## What it stores

Your settings (working hours for each weekday, shade colour, pattern, intensity, and whether shading is on) are saved with Chrome's built-in storage. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome syncs them across your browsers through your Google account, as it does for other extension settings. The developer has no access to them.

## What it doesn't do

- No network requests: the extension never contacts a server.
- No analytics, tracking, ads or cookies.
- No selling, sharing or transferring of data, because none is collected.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the update will appear here with a new date. Questions are welcome as an issue at [github.com/renegalindo/working-hours/issues](https://github.com/renegalindo/working-hours/issues).
